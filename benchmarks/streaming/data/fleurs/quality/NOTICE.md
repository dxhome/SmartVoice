# FLEURS-derived 90-case quality corpus

These 90 16 kHz mono PCM16 WAV files are derived from FLEURS, Google, Conneau et al. (2022), pinned at revision `d16ac437ac42fb543bf2893b934dac1af970e361`. The upstream dataset card states CC BY 4.0; attribution and exact sample checksums are in `../quality-corpus-v1.json`.

The corpus preserves 48 `new_confirmation` and 42 `existing_regression`/control records from the earlier sandbox evaluation. The variants include clean audio, quiet gain, 10 dB SNR noise, room-proxy echoes, silence and noise-only controls. Derived audio files remain covered by the source attribution; generated transformations and partition lineage are listed in the manifest.

Historical and current quality outputs are diagnostic or pending human review. They do not represent independent bilingual quality acceptance.
