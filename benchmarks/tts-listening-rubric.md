# TTS Blind Listening Rubric

Rate each audio sample independently. Keep the model mapping private until ratings are complete. Use the same playback device and comfortable, consistent volume. The prompt is included in the local rating sheet so intelligibility and pronunciation can be judged.

Use 1–5 for each dimension:

| Score | Naturalness | Intelligibility | Pronunciation and prosody |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unusable or strongly unnatural | Most words are difficult to understand | Frequent serious pronunciation or rhythm problems |
| 2 | Clearly synthetic and distracting | Many words need effort to understand | Several noticeable pronunciation or prosody problems |
| 3 | Understandable, with obvious unnaturalness | Main meaning is understandable, some words unclear | Some noticeable issues, but the sentence remains usable |
| 4 | Mostly natural, minor artifacts | Clear with only minor ambiguity | Mostly correct pronunciation and appropriate phrasing |
| 5 | Natural and comfortable to listen to | Clear throughout | Natural pronunciation, rhythm, and phrasing |

Leave a score blank when the audio is corrupted or cannot be rated, and note that in a separate private record. Do not put listener names or other personal data into the ratings template. The aggregate command reports mean scores and bootstrap intervals; it does not publish individual ratings.
